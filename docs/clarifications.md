# Clarifications

Questions I asked the Matic team in Slack and answers.

Each entry has the question, the assumption I stated when asking, the answer (verbatim), and what it means for the work. Design decisions and their trade-offs live in `docs/decisions.md` (Task 1) and in the spec under `docs/link-shortener/` (Task 2).

Status key: **Confirmed** (answer matches my assumption), **Changed** (answer overrides it), **My call** (left to me), **Unanswered** (my assumption stands).

## Task 1: Discounts

### 1. Can a rule span more than one product or the whole basket?

- **Assumption:** the design should allow cross-product rules, even though the two current ones are single-product.
- **Answer:** "Assume marketing team can come up with any new rules, including ones you invent."
- **Status:** Confirmed, and broader than I asked.
- **Implication:** a rule must be able to see the whole basket, not one product. Rule shapes cannot be enumerated in advance, so adding a rule has to mean writing new code without changing `Checkout`. I will add one invented rule of a different shape to prove this.

### 2. Who adds new rules: engineers in code, or marketing through configuration or a UI?

- **Assumption:** engineers add rules in code; a self-service DSL or admin UI is out of scope.
- **Answer:** "Your call. Whatever you think fits your version of MVP."
- **Status:** My call.
- **Implication:** rules are Ruby objects added by engineers. No DSL and no admin UI in the MVP; the reasoning and the conditions for revisiting it go in `docs/decisions.md`. The "interactive interface" nice-to-have is a small command-line script.


## Task 2: Link Shortener

### 1. What is the main use case and channel?

- **Assumption:** agents send links to customers by SMS during or after a call.
- **Answer:** "Initial use case is SMS to customers."
- **Status:** Confirmed.
- **Implication:** SMS is the first channel, so link length matters (message length and cost), but I think word "Initial" suggests other channels may follow, so nothing in the design should be SMS-only.

### 2. Who creates links and who opens them? Could links be posted publicly?

- **Assumption:** internal creation, public redirects, one link per customer rather than mass publication.
- **Answer:** "creation is internal only, redirects are public."
- **Status:** Confirmed. The social media part was not answered; I assume no.
- **Implication:** two surfaces with different exposure: a creation interface reachable only from internal systems, and a public redirect endpoint that needs abuse protection.

### 3. What volume should I plan for?

- **Assumption:** a few hundred agents, a few thousand links per day, a handful of clicks per link.
- **Answer:** "~1.5M links created per month."
- **Status:** Changed. This is roughly ten times or more above my assumption.
- **Implication:** about 50,000 links per day and about 18M per year. At this volume links are generated by systems, not typed by agents (see question 6). Clicks per link: I keep "a handful per link" and state it as an assumption in the spec. The spec needs a capacity estimate and a data retention policy.

### 4. Do the original URLs lead to sensitive data? Any expiry, guessability or audit requirements?

- **Assumption:** yes, so links expire and codes are not guessable.
- **Answer:** "Example: a customer's policy declarations page."
- **Status:** Confirmed on sensitivity. Expiry and audit were not answered; my assumption stands.
- **Implication:** a short link can lead to a customer's personal insurance documents, so the short code is effectively an access path. Codes must not be guessable or enumerable, I think it make sense to propose expiry and audit rules in the spec and flag them for confirmation.

### 5. Is click tracking or reporting needed?

- **Assumption:** basic click counts per link and per agent.
- **Answer:** "Up to you. Propose what you think would be useful and why."
- **Status:** My call.
- **Implication:** the spec must include a tracking proposal with a justification for each metric, and say what is deliberately left out.

### 6. How are links created: web UI or API?

- **Assumption:** API first, plus a minimal UI.
- **Answer:** "Context: when we generate an SMS, the text can contain zero or more links that need shortening. What interface would you suggest for that?"
- **Status:** Changed, and returned to me as a design question.
- **Implication:** the primary consumer is the system that generates SMS text, not a person. The interface has to handle a message containing zero, one or several links. Choosing and justifying that interface is important part of the spec. A UI for agents is no longer an MVP requirement.

### 7. What does "cost" mean, and is an off-the-shelf service an option?

- **Assumption:** both engineering effort and running costs, with a short build vs. buy comparison.
- **Answer:** "Build vs. buy"
- **Status:** Confirmed on the comparison. Effort versus running cost was not specified; I cover both.
- **Implication:** the cost section is framed as build versus buy, priced at 1.5M links per month, with a recommendation.

### 8. Any constraints on stack, cloud, domain or deadline?

- **Assumption:** current Ruby stack and cloud, a new short domain, no hard deadline.
- **Answer:** "No constraints. Choose whatever stack and infrastructure you think fits best."
- **Status:** My call.
- **Implication:** stack and infrastructure are my choice and need a stated reason in the spec. No target date was given, so the rollout plan uses phases and durations, not calendar dates.
